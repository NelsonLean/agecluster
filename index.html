    ▸ Data quality

    Standard age groups follow 5-year bands up to 29, then 10-year bands; the alternative grouping (chosen in the Age card) applies everywhere, and “30–60” includes 60-year-olds. Facility type is assigned by keywords in the facility name (hospital, emergency, ETD, KK or Klinik Kesihatan for public clinics, other klinik names as private clinics, farmasi, and so on); free-text answers that are not a facility are grouped as “No facility visit” or “Other or unclear”.
